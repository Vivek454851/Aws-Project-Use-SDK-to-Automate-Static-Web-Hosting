Our Story

What is Rangoli?

Rangoli is one of the oldest Indian art forms, traditionally created on floors during festivals and special occasions. The word comes from Sanskrit — "Rang" (color) and "Aavalli" (row of colors).

It is an expression of joy, beauty, and auspiciousness drawn during festivals like Diwali, Onam, Navratri, and Pongal. Each region of India has its own style — from Kerala's Onam Pookalam to Tamil Nadu's Kolam.
